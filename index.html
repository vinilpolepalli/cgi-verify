Methodology, assumptions & provenance

  • Inputs: per-provider prices, smoothed votes, stability bands, and liveness-based aggregation weights as published by CGI (method id annex_a_v0_2_calc_v17, weight cap 0.3, floor 0.025). Published live weights sum to 1; missing providers carry no weight.
  • Assumption: the page documents “fault-tolerant, outlier-resistant” aggregation but not the exact estimator. Three candidates are computed over live providers: weighted mean, lower weighted median, and interpolated weighted median (cumulative weight at vote midpoints). The headline defaults to the interpolated median because it is outlier-resistant and is within about 0.6% on B300, but no single candidate is closest on every SKU (e.g. the lower median is nearer on B200, the mean on H100) and none match the published value exactly.
  • Not modelled: the published payload reports 3 “votes” per provider (e.g. 18 for 6 providers on B300) and a stability band; the way votes are formed from bands is not documented on the page, so it is not reproduced. Weight cap/floor re-application after a stress scenario is also not modelled; weights are simply renormalized over remaining live providers.
  • Derived figures: 1D / 30D change come from the published daily series; high/low are the max/min live provider price. 1D change and the B300 table were cross-checked against the visible page at fetch time.
